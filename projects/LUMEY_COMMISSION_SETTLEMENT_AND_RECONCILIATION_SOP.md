# LUMEY ENERGY — COMMISSION SETTLEMENT & RECONCILIATION SOP
## Standard Operating Procedure for Weekly Tuesday Affiliate Payouts & Audit Controls
*(Finance, Operations & Partner Relations Departments)*

---

## 1. PURPOSE & TIMELINE OVERVIEW
To ensure 100% affiliate partner trust, eliminate payout delays, and maintain bulletproof financial records, all affiliate commissions are reconciled and paid on a strict weekly cycle:

```
┌────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┐
│        MONDAY (5:00 PM)        │       TUESDAY (10:00 AM)       │       TUESDAY (1:00 PM)       │
│     Weekly Cut-off & Audit     │     Batch Banking Prep & NUBAN │   Bulk Transfer Execution &   │
│     Waybill delivery confirmed │     1st-Sale Bonus verification│   WhatsApp Alert Distribution │
└────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. ELIGIBILITY & COMMISSION TIER AUDIT RULES

### Rule 1: Delivered Order Requirement
Commissions are only accrued and disbursed for orders that have been **delivered and confirmed by the customer** between Monday 00:00:00 and Sunday 23:59:59 of the preceding week. Pending, in-transit, or cancelled waybills carry over to the subsequent settlement cycle.

### Rule 2: Minimum Payout Threshold
* The minimum payout threshold is **₦10,000**.
* If an affiliate’s total weekly commission is less than ₦10,000, it automatically rolls forward into their cumulative ledger balance until the threshold is met.

### Rule 3: Tier Determination & Automatic Upgrade
Commissions are calculated based on monthly gross sales volume generated by the partner:
* **Starter Tier (5.0%):** ₦250k – ₦749k monthly sales
* **Active Tier (7.5%):** ₦750k – ₦1.99M monthly sales
* **Pro Tier (10.0%):** ₦2.0M – ₦4.99M monthly sales
* **Ambassador Tier (12.0%):** ₦5.0M+ monthly sales

*Note:* When an affiliate's cumulative monthly sales cross into a higher tier during a billing cycle, all subsequent sales in that calendar month are calculated at the higher percentage.

### Rule 4: The ₦5,000 First-Sale Accelerator Bonus
Every new affiliate who closes their **first confirmed PowerBox sale within 14 calendar days of registration** receives an automatic **₦5,000 cash bonus** credited to their Tuesday payout.

## 3. STEP-BY-STEP RECONCILIATION WORKFLOW

### Step 1: Export Pending Orders & Partner Ledger
Every Monday at 5:00 PM:
1. Open the Lumey Admin Database or run `.\reconcile_payouts.ps1`.
2. Extract all orders flagged as `Confirmed` or `Delivered` with valid voucher codes (`LUMEY-[HANDLE]`).
3. Cross-reference the order against waybill delivery confirmation notes from logistics partners (GIG Logistics, Peace Mass Transit, Interstate transport manifests).

### Step 2: Bank NUBAN Name Resolution
Every Tuesday at 10:00 AM:
1. Filter the batch for active NUBAN accounts (10 digits) and verified Nigerian financial institutions (Opay, Moniepoint, GTBank, Access Bank, Zenith Bank, Kuda, etc.).
2. Confirm the account name resolved matches the registered affiliate's KYC identity.
3. Export the formatted **Bulk Payment CSV**:
   ```csv
   Beneficiary_NUBAN,Bank_Code,Beneficiary_Name,Amount_NGN,Narration
   0123456789,058,CHUKWUMA ADEBAYO,55000.00,Lumey Commission LUMEY-CHUKS
   ```

### Step 3: Executive Sign-off & Disbursement
Every Tuesday at 1:00 PM:
1. The Finance Officer uploads the batch payment file to Lumey's corporate banking portal (Moniepoint Business or Commercial Internet Banking).
2. The Managing Director / Finance Director authorizes the batch transfer with two-factor authentication (2FA token).
3. System logs the unique Central Bank / NIP session reference ID for every completed payout.

### Step 4: WhatsApp Payment Confirmation Alert
Every Tuesday before 2:00 PM:
An automated WhatsApp broadcast is triggered to each paid affiliate:

```text
🎉 TUESDAY PAYOUT CONFIRMATION!

Hello [AFFILIATE_NAME],
Your commission payout of ₦[AMOUNT] has been transferred to your [BANK_NAME] account ([NUBAN])!

• Settlement Period: [DATE_RANGE]
• Reference ID: [NIP_SESSION_ID]

📸 Share your credit alert on your WhatsApp Status and tag us to unlock the Pro Partner badge!
Keep shining and powering Nigeria! ⚡
```

## 4. HANDLING REFUNDS, DEFECTS & FRAUD

* **Customer Cancellations:** If an order is cancelled or rejected prior to delivery, no commission is accrued.
* **Return within 7 Days:** If a customer initiates a verified return due to a manufacturing defect, the partner's commission is preserved if the unit is swapped under warranty. If an outright cash refund is issued, the commission amount is deducted from the partner's future accruals.
* **Self-Referral Prohibition:** Partners may not create dummy buyer accounts to purchase units for personal use simply to claim commissions on single unapproved orders without prior authorization.
