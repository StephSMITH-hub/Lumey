PART 1: THE SCIENCE & ART OF HIGH-CONVERTING FLYER COPYWRITING

To write flyer copy that stops people in their tracks—whether printed on physical cardstock or posted as a 1080x1080 square image on WhatsApp, Instagram, X, or Facebook—you must understand how human psychology interacts with visual media.

1. The 3-Second Visual Hierarchy Rule

A prospect scrolling through social media feeds or glancing at a printed flyer gives you under 3 seconds before deciding to engage or look away:

  • Level 1: The Visual Pattern Interrupt (0–1 second): High-contrast color palette (Dark Emerald Green, Deep Obsidian, Metallic Gold accents, and Stark White text). Avoid washed-out pastels or generic low-contrast backgrounds.
  • Level 2: The Focal Headline (1–2 seconds): A bold, punchy claim that agitates a visceral pain point or promises a specific financial/lifestyle outcome. Never write "Welcome to Lumey Energy". Write: "STOP ENTERTAINING 500 STATUS VIEWERS FOR FREE" or "REST IN PEACE, PETROL GENERATOR".
  • Level 3: Micro-Bullets & Value Stack (2–3 seconds): Maximum of 3 to 5 bullet points. Each bullet must deliver a concrete benefit, not just a technical spec (e.g., "Tabletop fridge (120L) runs all day" instead of "300W inductive capability").
  • Level 4: Trust Seals & Risk Reversals: Badges like "₦0 Capital Required", "5-Year Official Battery Warranty", "Tuesday Direct Bank Payouts", and "Grade-A LiFePO4 Chemistry".
  • Level 5: Unambiguous Call to Action (CTA): Single clear next step with an immediate incentive: "Use Code [PARTNER_CODE] for ₦5,000 OFF" and "Apply free at lumeyenergy.com/affiliate".

  • Enemy Framing: In Nigeria today, people are exhausted by the "Fuel Tax" (₦1,200/litre petrol) and erratic Band A electricity tariffs. Position the fuel generator and grid collapse as the enemy that Lumey's silent solar boxes eliminate.
