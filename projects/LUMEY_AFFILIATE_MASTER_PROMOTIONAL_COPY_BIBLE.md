# PART 1: THE SCIENCE & ART OF HIGH-CONVERTING FLYER COPYWRITING

To write flyer copy that stops people in their tracks—whether printed on physical cardstock or posted as a 1080x1080 image on WhatsApp, Instagram, X, or Facebook—you must understand how human psychology interacts with visual media.

### 1. The 3-Second Visual Hierarchy Rule
A prospect scrolling on social media or glancing at a flyer gives you **3 seconds** before deciding to engage or look away.
* **Level 1: The Visual Pattern Interrupt (0–1 second):** High-contrast color palette (Dark Emerald Green, Deep Obsidian, Metallic Gold accents, Stark White text). No washed-out colors.
* **Level 2: The Focal Headline (1–2 seconds):** A bold, punchy claim that agitates a visceral pain point or promises a specific financial/lifestyle outcome. Never write "Welcome to Lumey Energy". Write: *"STOP ENTERTAINING 500 STATUS VIEWERS FOR FREE"* or *"REST IN PEACE, PETROL GENERATOR"*.
* **Level 3: Micro-Bullets & Value Stack (2–3 seconds):** Maximum of 3 to 5 bullet points. Each bullet must deliver a concrete benefit, not just a technical spec. (e.g., *"Tabletop fridge (120L) runs all day"* instead of *"300W inductive capability"*).
* **Level 4: Trust Seals & Risk Reversals:** Badges like *"₦0 Capital Required"*, *"5-Year Official Warranty"*, *"Tuesday Direct Bank Payouts"*, *"Grade-A LiFePO4 Battery"*.
* **Level 5: Unambiguous Call to Action (CTA):** Single clear next step with an immediate incentive: *"Use Code [PARTNER_CODE] for ₦5,000 OFF"* and *"Apply free at lumeyenergy.com/affiliate"*.

### 2. The 4 Direct-Response Copywriting Pillars for Nigeria (August 2026)
1. **Enemy Framing:** In Nigeria today, people are exhausted by the "Fuel Tax" (₦1,200/litre petrol) and erratic Band A electricity tariffs. Position the fuel generator and grid collapse as the enemy that Lumey's silent solar boxes eliminate.
2. **Loss Aversion (Pain > Gain):** Psychological research shows people will fight twice as hard to avoid losing something as they will to gain it. Highlighting *"Don't let ₦150k in meat spoil in your freezer"* converts 300% better than *"Keep your freezer cold"*.
3. **Specific Arithmetic:** Vague claims like *"Make good money"* get ignored. Concrete numbers like *"Sell 2x PB 1500 + 1x PB 2600 = ₦261,000 in your bank account before month ends"* trigger instant belief and motivation.
4. **The Scarcity & Voucher Hook:** Giving the affiliate an exclusive coupon code (`[PARTNER_CODE]`) that saves buyers ₦5,000 creates instant social reciprocity. The prospect feels they are getting a secret VIP hook.
