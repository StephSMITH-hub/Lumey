Overview 1. The Two-Path Commercial Architecture

Nigeria's energy crisis has intensified: PMS (petrol) floats between ₦1,000 and ₦1,250+ per litre, and Band A electricity tariffs exceed ₦209–₦225/kWh. Nigerian households, professionals, and shop owners are demanding immediate, quiet, zero-fuel alternatives.

The Affiliate Track ("Zero Capital, 100% Hustle")

Designed for university students, NYSC corpers, remote tech professionals, digital creators, and social media influencers.

  • Capital Requirement: ₦0 (Zero Capital, no registration fee).
  • Inventory Risk: Zero. Lumey warehouses, insures, and ships all products.
  • Earnings: 5% to 12% cash commission paid weekly every Tuesday.
  • First-Sale Incentive: Instant ₦5,000 cash bonus on first closed order.

The Distributor Track ("Capital, Inventory, Retail")

Designed for electrical shops, solar installers, estate facility managers, and electronics retailers with physical storefronts.

  • Capital Requirement: Minimum ₦500,000 opening inventory order.
  • Gross Margins: 15% to 22% upfront wholesale discount.
  • Territorial Protection: Exclusive LGA or Statewide distribution rights.
  • Quarterly Rebates: Additional 2% to 7% credit notes for volume targets.
