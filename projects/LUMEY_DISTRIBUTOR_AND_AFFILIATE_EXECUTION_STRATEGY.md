### Operational Steps for Distributors:
* **Step 1 (Application & Document Ingestion):**
  * Applicant completes Distributor Questionnaire: Business registration (CAC), 2 years bank statements, photos of current storefront/showroom, proposed storage security.
* **Step 2 (Site Verification):**
  * Lumey Regional Field Manager visits the location within 5 business days:
    - Verifies foot traffic, road accessibility, clean display window, grid stability, and secure warehousing.
    - Signs official Distributor Agreement specifying assigned exclusive LGA/territory and 60-day reorder covenant.
* **Step 3 (Financial Commitment):**
  * Distributor transfers 50% deposit for initial opening inventory (minimum ₦500k for Reseller, ₦2M for Regional, ₦5M for Master).
  * Remaining 50% cleared upon bill of lading generation prior to depot dispatch. First 3 orders must be 100% prepaid before credit lines are unlocked.
* **Step 4 (Physical Merchandising & Branding Kit Deployment):**
  * Lumey dispatches the **Physical Showroom Pack**:
    - 1x Branded 2m x 85cm Roll-Up Luxury Aluminum Display Banner.

    - 1x Serialized Branded Carbonized Triplicate Sales & Warranty Receipt Book.
    - Official Acrylic "Lumey Authorized Experience Center" Counter Plaque.
* **Step 5 (Sales & Technical Certification Day):**
  * Lumey Technical Sales Trainer conducts a 4-hour in-person or intensive interactive workshop with the distributor's sales reps:
    - *Physical unboxing and setup.*
    - *Solar panel MC4 cabling, safety switches, and earthing.*
    - *Live Load Demonstration execution (The Kettle/Iron Test).*
    - *Customer installment screening and BVN/guarantor protocol.*

  * Lumey Regional Sales Executive is physically present in the distributor's showroom for the first 48 hours of launch.

# PART 4: INSTALLMENT & FINANCING EXECUTION BLUEPRINT

Customer financing is the single greatest lever to unlock 5x sales volume in Nigeria, but it carries existential credit risk if poorly structured. Lumey operates a strict, zero-bad-debt financing architecture.

### 4.1 The Golden Rule of Financing
> **"NEVER RELEASE PHYSICAL PRODUCT TO A CUSTOMER OR DISTRIBUTOR WITHOUT AT LEAST A 30% UPFRONT DEPOSIT AND FULLY VERIFIED IDENTITY."**

---

### 4.2 Customer Installment Rules (Via Distributors)
Distributors have the discretion to offer installment plans directly to their retail walk-in customers. However:
1. **Lumey gets paid 100% upfront:** When a distributor sells a unit on installments, Lumey is paid in full at wholesale price. The distributor carries the retail customer credit risk. This protects Lumey's manufacturing cash flow while allowing distributors to build a lucrative local financing portfolio.
2. **The 30% Minimum Down Payment:** No unit ever leaves the showroom floor with less than 30% cleared funds.
3. **Strict 90-Day Maximum Term:** Installment tenures are strictly capped at 3 months (3 equal monthly installments). Long terms create default nightmares.
4. **Mandatory KYC Dossier:**
   * Customer Bank Verification Number (BVN) and National Identity Number (NIN).
   * Verifiable proof of residential address (current utility bill or estate security gate pass).
   * Two (2) verifiable personal guarantors with verified phone numbers and workplace addresses.
5. **The Warranty Card Collateral Mechanism:**
   * The distributor retains physical possession of the official **Serialized Lumey Warranty Certificate & Proof of Ownership Card** in their safe.
   * The customer is informed in writing: *The manufacturer warranty and after-sales service desk are locked until final installment balance clears.*
   * In the event of default, the distributor initiates repossession. Lumey does not engage in retail consumer debt collection.
