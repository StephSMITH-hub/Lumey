## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & COMMERCIAL OBJECTIVES

Lumey Energy's commercial strategy is engineered to dominate Nigeria's multi-billion-naira decentralized power market. With national grid instability, Band A tariffs exceeding ₦209–₦225/kWh, and petrol (PMS) prices fluctuating between ₦1,000 and ₦1,250+ per litre, the demand for silent, zero-fuel, zero-maintenance power stations is at an all-time peak.

To capture market share rapidly across Nigeria's 36 states and the FCT without committing capital to hundreds of company-owned brick-and-mortar stores, Lumey employs a high-velocity **Two-Path Decentralized Distribution Engine**:

1. **The Affiliate Track ("Zero Capital, 100% Hustle"):** Empowering ambitious youth, remote tech workers, corporate professionals, and digital creators to generate sales with zero upfront inventory, earning 5% to 12% cash commissions with dropshipped fulfillment managed directly by Lumey.
2. **The Distributor Track ("Capital, Inventory, Retail"):** Partnering with established electrical shops, solar installers, estate facility managers, and electronics retailers who purchase inventory at 15% to 22% wholesale discounts, backed by territorial exclusivity and regional depot stock support.

This document details the **complete master inventory of deliverables** required to operationalize this distribution model, turning high-level business strategy into immediate, tangible commercial assets, legal instruments, marketing packs, and operating systems.
