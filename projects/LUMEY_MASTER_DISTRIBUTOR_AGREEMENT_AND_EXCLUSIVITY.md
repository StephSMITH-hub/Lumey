## CLAUSE 3: TERRITORIAL RIGHTS & THE 60-DAY EXCLUSIVITY COVENANT

3.1. **Grant of Territory Exclusivity:** For partners appointed as **Regional Distributor** (LGA-level exclusivity) or **Master Distributor** (State-level exclusivity), the Company covenants not to appoint or supply any other stocking commercial distributor within the perimeter defined in **Schedule A** for the duration of this Agreement, subject to Clause 3.2.

3.2. **The 60-Day Active Reorder Covenant:**  
(a) To maintain territorial exclusivity, the Distributor must execute a qualifying restocking order at least once every **sixty (60) calendar days**.  
(b) If the Distributor fails to place an active restocking order meeting the minimum tier threshold within any sixty (60) consecutive calendar day period, the territorial exclusivity shall automatically terminate without requiring written notice. Upon such lapse, the Company reserves the immediate, unfettered right to appoint alternative distributors within that geographic perimeter.

3.3. **Online & Direct Sales Carve-out:** The Company retains the right to operate its national website (`lumeyenergy.com`) and social media marketing campaigns. However, customer orders originating from the Distributor's exclusive perimeter that request local pickup or over-the-counter collection shall be routed to the Distributor.

## CLAUSE 5: LOGISTICS, REGIONAL DEPOTS & FREIGHT

5.1. **Factory & Regional Depot Network:** Stock dispatches shall originate from either the Central Factory (Oyemekun, Akure, Ondo State) or the closest regional stocking hub:
* **Lagos Depot:** Ikeja & Alaba access hub.
* **Abuja Depot:** FCT central logistics zone.
* **Port Harcourt Depot:** South-South fulfillment terminal.
* **Kano Depot:** Northern regional clearing hub.
* **Ibadan Depot:** South-West zone.

5.2. **Packaging & Shockproof Transit:** All PowerBoxes are crated in high-density industrial foam and wooden reinforced frames. Waybills shall be dispatched within twenty-four (24) business hours of confirmed funds receipt.

5.3. **In-Transit Risk & Waybill Inspection:** Risk of transit loss transfers upon handover to verified transport partners. The Distributor must inspect all crates upon delivery and record any exterior structural damage on the carrier waybill slip within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt.

## CLAUSE 6: MINIMUM ADVERTISED PRICE (MAP) & ANTI-UNDERCUTTING

6.1. **Strict Price Integrity:** The Distributor expressly agrees that it shall not advertise, display, list, or market any Lumey PowerBox model at a price lower than the official catalogue retail price established by the Company.

6.2. **Zero-Tolerance Penalty:** Any distributor found guilty of predatory undercutting, dumping stock, or publicly advertising discounted rates that compromise the commercial viability of neighboring distributors shall receive an immediate written reprimand. A second verified infraction results in **instant cancellation of distributorship, forfeiture of territorial exclusivity, and refusal of future wholesale orders**.

## CLAUSE 8: OFFICIAL WARRANTIES & RMA PROTOCOL

8.1. **Standard Warranty Schedule:**
* **Hybrid Pure Sine Wave Inverters:** Twelve (12) calendar months from the date of retail sale.
* **LiFePO4 Lithium Batteries:** Five (5) calendar years (3,500+ cycles) against manufacturing cell degradation.

8.2. **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Workflow:**
* **Step 1 (Triage):** Distributor technician executes the 5-point intake diagnostic checklist.
* **Step 2 (Depot Submission):** Defective unit logged with serial number and returned to regional depot.
* **Step 3 (Diagnostic SLA):** Company depot completes diagnosis within forty-eight (48) hours.
* **Step 4 (Resolution):** Component replacement or complete unit swap provided within seven (7) business days.

8.3. **Exclusions:** Warranty is void if the seal is broken by non-certified technicians, or if failure is caused by physical water immersion, lightning strike without mandatory surge protector, or external short circuit.

## CLAUSE 9: TERM, TERMINATION & EXIT

9.1. **Term:** This Agreement is valid for an initial period of twelve (12) calendar months from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive 12-month periods upon satisfaction of the Annual Volume Target.

9.2. **Termination for Cause:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if:
* The other Party commits a material breach (including MAP violations or counterfeit substitution) and fails to remedy within fourteen (14) calendar days.
* The Distributor becomes insolvent, enters liquidation, or ceases commercial retail operations.
